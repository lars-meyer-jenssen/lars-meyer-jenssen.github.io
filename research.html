Research

Research Interests & Projects

My research brings together psychological perspectives on emotion and motivation with empirical research on professional competence across early childhood and school education.

Research Interests

Research area 01

Emotional and Motivational Processes in Education

My research investigates emotional and motivational processes in educational settings, with a particular focus on achievement emotions, self-conscious emotions such as shame and pride, self-concept, self-efficacy, and control-value appraisals. I examine their antecedents, development, and consequences for learning and teaching.

Research area 02

Professional Competence of Educators and Teachers

My research examines the antecedents, structure, development, and consequences of professional competence among early childhood educators and school teachers. Drawing on a competence continuum perspective, I investigate professional dispositions (including professional knowledge), situation-specific skills, and observable teaching behavior.

Much of my research is situated in mathematics education, particularly in early childhood education, primary education, and teacher education. My research also addresses broader educational and psychological questions across different educational contexts.

Independent Research Funding

2025–2028 · Principal Investigator

PriSMa-P

Pre-service primary teachers’ shame and pride experienced in mathematics when working at school

Funded by: German Research Foundation (Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft, DFG). Independently acquired funding. DFG project number: 545764321.

The project investigates shame and pride among pre-service primary school teachers during their teaching practicum. Using longitudinal data, it examines the antecedents, development, and consequences of these emotions in learning and teaching mathematics.

2021–2022 · Principal Investigator

PriSMa

Shame and Pride in Mathematics among Pre-Service Primary School Teachers

Funded by: Berlin University Alliance (BUA). Independently acquired funding. BUA funding reference: 420_CDA_4.

The project investigated shame and pride in mathematics among pre-service primary school teachers, primarily during their bachelor's studies. It focused on the antecedents and consequences of these emotions and provided a foundation for subsequent research on achievement emotions in teacher education.

Collaborative Research Projects

2016–2019 · Project Coordinator

Pro-KomMa

Professionalization of Early Childhood Teacher Education: Convergent, Discriminant, and Predictive Validation of KomMa Models and Instruments

Funded by: German Federal Ministry of Education and Research (Bundesministerium für Bildung und Forschung, BMBF).

The project examined the validity of competence models and assessment instruments developed in KomMa, including their predictive relevance for children's mathematical development.

2012–2015 · Research Associate

KomMa

Structure, Level, and Modeling of Early Childhood Educators’ Professional Competence in Mathematics

Funded by: German Federal Ministry of Education and Research (BMBF).

The project investigated the structure and level of early childhood educators’ professional competence in mathematics, including the development and validation of assessment instruments.

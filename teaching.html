Teaching Philosophy

My teaching is grounded in evidence-based educational psychology and informed by my practical experience as a psychological psychotherapist. I aim to connect scientific knowledge with educational and clinical practice, helping students understand how psychological theories and findings can inform professional decisions. To support this, I emphasize autonomy-supportive learning environments, transparent learning objectives, and opportunities for active engagement and reflection. Through research-based and experiential learning, I seek to foster students’ critical thinking and their ability to apply psychological knowledge in professional contexts.
